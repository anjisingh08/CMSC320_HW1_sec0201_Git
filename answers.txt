Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: We might consider using git because we may want to keep track of
coding projects in an organized way. In other contexts, guthub is used to share
code and projects as well (for collaboration).

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: For me personally, I am interested in applying data science to biology
research. For example, in my research lab, I am using data science to track and
analyze fluorescence in human macrophage cells infected with mycobacterium 
tuberculosis.
